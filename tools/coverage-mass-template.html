Estimated coverage mass

Approximate student working time for one pass through MVP and regular Khan Integrated Math I–III. Every listed Khan video and article contributes; practice uses a stated question-count scenario. These are exploratory time budgets, not measured learning time.

How to read this: Each percentage is a topic’s estimated time divided by the total estimated time for that curriculum. This compares relative emphasis even when the curricula have different total workloads. Guided and practice percentages use the same whole-curriculum denominator and add to the topic’s share. Shared material is divided among its mapped broad topics. Time ranges remain exploratory assumptions, not confidence intervals for these percentages.

Assumptions, calibration, and limitations

Nine Khan player durations were inspected: median minutes. The remaining videos use that median, with a 25% pause allowance and a broad 2.3–15 minute working-time range. Articles use 5 minutes (2–10). Practice defaults to four questions per exercise skill at 3 minutes each (range 1.5–6 minutes per question). This is a scenario, not Khan’s required adaptive workload.

MVP defaults to 50 minutes per Main component (25–90) and 24 per Ready, Set, or Go component (12–48). Nine homework components in Math I 3.1, 3.2, and 3.6 have allowances informed by inspected problem loads. All time allowances remain judgments; none were observed from students. Main includes guided classroom work, including practice tasks. MVP task budgets may be particularly inaccurate for long tasks and proofs.

Topic allocation uses existing map references and Khan lesson-group tags. Equal shares are a simplifying assumption; individual activities have not all been classified. Finer subskills covered by a broader MVP entry cannot yet be assigned reliable independent budgets. An empty MVP allocation means absent from the current map, pending packet-level checking. Empty Khan allocation means no match in the regular Integrated courses, not absence everywhere on Khan.

Quizzes, unit tests, course challenges, retries, and supplemental resources are excluded. Exact duplicate Khan URLs count once; repeats with distinct course URLs count as another pass. Background or unmapped activities have a separate bucket. Preserve the original maps for location evidence.
