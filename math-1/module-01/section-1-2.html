Worked example — Ready 3

Original problem: Find \(f(-4)\) for \(f(n)=8n-3\).

\[f(-4)=8(-4)-3=-32-3=-35.\]

The input is −4; the output is −35. Parentheses help keep the negative sign attached to the input.
