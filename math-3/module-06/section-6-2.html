Try the packet task on your own first

Attempt the task before reading the worked examples. Use the explanations to check your reasoning or get help when stuck.

In your MVP packetMain 1–4 · pages 5–6

Turn elapsed time into an angle

A period is the time for one complete repeat. This wheel completes one counterclockwise turn in 20 seconds, so it turns \(360^\circ/20=18^\circ\) per second. Time is measured from A, the rightmost point, level with the center.

\[\text{angle turned}=18t\text{ degrees}.\]

During the first five seconds the rider is in the upper-right quarter of the wheel. A right triangle gives the height:

\[h(t)=30+25\sin(18t^\circ),\qquad 0\le t\le5.\]

Here \(18t^\circ\) means an angle of \(18t\) degrees. This first formula uses the acute-angle sine ratio. For positions in other quarters, use a reference angle: the acute angle between the spoke and the horizontal line through the center. Use symmetry and add the vertical distance above the center or subtract it below the center.

Worked example — Main 2, 8 seconds

Original problem: Find the height 8 seconds after A.

\[18(8)=144^\circ,\qquad \alpha=180^\circ-144^\circ=36^\circ.\]

The rider is in the upper-left quarter, so add the triangle’s vertical side to 30:

\[h=30+25\sin36^\circ\approx44.69\text{ feet}.\]

At 18 seconds the angle is 324°, so the same 36° reference triangle is below the center. The height is \(30-25\sin36^\circ\approx15.31\) feet.

Repeated positions have repeated heights

Subtract a full 20-second turn when a time exceeds 20 seconds. For example, 23 seconds and 3 seconds give the same position. At 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 seconds, the heights are 30, 55, 30, 5 and 30 feet.

A function is positive where its graph is above the x-axis, and negative where it is below. A zero belongs to neither group. Include a filled endpoint only when its output has the required sign. For the line pieces in 5–6, find each slope separately and state the input interval for each rule.

4. Go — Find missing angles

In your MVP packetGo 13–18 · page 10

Work independently first. Use the answers below to check your work after you have tried each problem, or to help if you are stuck.

Side \(a\) is opposite angle A, and \(c\) is the hypotenuse because C is the right angle. Use \(A=\sin^{-1}(a/c)\) when a and c are known, or \(A=\tan^{-1}(a/b)\) when both legs are known. Here \(\sin^{-1}\) means inverse sine: the calculator finds an angle from a ratio. It does not mean the reciprocal of sine. Use degree mode and \(B=90^\circ-A\).

Teacher/parent review of the source packet

Set 8 asks for a right triangle at every listed time, but at 15 and 30 seconds the vertical leg is zero. Those positions lie on the horizontal diameter and do not form a nondegenerate triangle. Use the center height, 30 feet. Go 16 prints “18.cm”; interpreted as 18 cm.
