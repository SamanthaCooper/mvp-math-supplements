Sine does not distribute across addition

For example, \(\sin(30^\circ+30^\circ)=\sqrt3/2\), whereas \(\sin30^\circ+\sin30^\circ=1\). The correct formulas account for both coordinates of the rotated point:

\[\sin(a+b)=\sin a\cos b+\cos a\sin b\]
\[\cos(a+b)=\cos a\cos b-\sin a\sin b\]
\[\sin(a-b)=\sin a\cos b-\cos a\sin b\]
\[\cos(a-b)=\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b\]

One way to understand the first two formulas is to break the second unit-length ray into a component \(\cos b\) along the ray at angle \(a\), and a component \(\sin b\) perpendicular to it. Their vertical contributions are \(\cos b\sin a\) and \(\sin b\cos a\); their horizontal contributions are \(\cos b\cos a\) and \(-\sin b\sin a\). Add the contributions. This is the same component reasoning behind the packet’s rectangle diagram.

For subtraction, replace \(b\) with \(-b\) and use sine’s oddness and cosine’s evenness. The different signs follow from those facts.

Worked example — Set 19

Original problem: Find \(\sin75^\circ\) exactly.

Worked solution: Write 75° as 45°+30°, then substitute known unit-circle values:

\[\sin75^\circ=\sin45^\circ\cos30^\circ+\cos45^\circ\sin30^\circ=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\frac{\sqrt3}{2}+\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\frac12=\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}.\]

Double-angle formulas are addition formulas with equal angles

\[\sin(2a)=2\sin a\cos a\]
\[\cos(2a)=\cos^2a-\sin^2a=1-2\sin^2a=2\cos^2a-1\]

For example, if \(\sin a=3/5\) and \(a\) is in Quadrant II, then \(\cos a=-4/5\), so \(\sin2a=-24/25\) and \(\cos2a=7/25\). The quadrant determines the missing sign before you apply the formulas.
